## Objective
**Observable behavior:** an owner-approved same-provider retry and a separately approved destination change each produce distinct current ACE decisions, final-send hashes and correlated provider responses; a denied attempt has zero external sends.
**Non-goals:** no experiment to discover provider behavior without approval, no arbitrary retries, no private source, no benchmark savings claim.
**Definition of accepted:** raw live provider traces and final-send records identify each physical attempt and response, with all failures retained and no route/model drift.
