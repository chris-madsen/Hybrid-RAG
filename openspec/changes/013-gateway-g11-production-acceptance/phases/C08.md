## Objective
**Observable behavior:** paired native-Hermes vs Gateway-optional observations report causal ACE delivery only when an ACE Gateway request/receipt is corroborated by independently verified final-send evidence. Quality, failures, token subsets, costs and unknowns remain correct.
**Non-goals:** no Codex/retired-tool four-arm substitution, no second Harbor accounting domain, no synthetic zero counters, no paid run or savings conclusion.
**Definition of accepted:** raw trials are bound to the frozen task manifest; receipt-to-final-send correlation is explicit; quality regression/failed trial remains in denominator; unknown usage/cost remains unknown and blocks causal ratio.
