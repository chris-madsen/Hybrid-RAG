## Objective
**Observable behavior:** exactly one disposable synthetic/public-data, non-stream Chat request traverses the approved pinned Bifröst route and receives a response from the owner-selected real provider/model; its final-send evidence correlates to the ACE authorization and response.
**Non-goals:** no model/provider choice by the agent, no private source, no fallback/retry experiment, no stream test, no production promotion or token-savings claim.
**Definition of accepted:** C01 mock proof exists; current route/config and provider identity are bound; a human authorizes destination, data class and spending; final send and real response correlate; raw restricted data stays outside the checkout.
